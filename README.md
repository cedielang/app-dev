# 🎮 VALORANT: A 5v5 tactical shooter featuring agents with unique abilities 
---
## 👁️‍🗨️ Overview
#### Valorant is set in a near-future Earth and features a team-based gameplay structure where two teams of five players compete against each other. Players select from a roster of Agents, each with unique abilities that can influence the outcome of matches. The main objective is to either plant or defuse a bomb-like device called the Spike within a set time limit, or eliminate the opposing team.
---
## 🕹️ Gameplay Mechanics
- **Rounds**: Each match consists of multiple rounds, with teams alternating between attacking and defending. The first team to win 13 rounds wins the match.
- **Economy System**: Players earn in-game currency (Creds) based on their performance in previous rounds, which can be used to purchase weapons, armor, and abilities at the start of each round.
- **Agent Abilities**: Each Agent has a set of abilities, including a signature ability and an ultimate ability that can turn the tide of battle. Players must strategically use these abilities in conjunction with their gunplay to succeed.
---
## 🤖 Agent and Roles
### As of October 2025, there are 27 Agents in Valorant, categorized into four roles:
- **Duelists**: Aggressive characters designed to secure kills and create space for their team.
- **Sentinels**: Defensive agents that provide support and healing to teammates.
- **Initiators**: Agents that disrupt enemy defenses and create opportunities for their team.
- **Controllers**: Characters that manipulate the battlefield, using abilities to block vision and control areas.
---
## ⚙️ Development and Platforms
#### Valorant was officially released on June 2, 2020, for Windows, with subsequent ports to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S in June 2024. A mobile version, Valorant Mobile, launched in China on August 19, 2025, with a global release planned for later.
